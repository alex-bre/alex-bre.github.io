Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

This is a static website with no server component of its own, no accounts, and no login. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. All files, including the fonts, are served from this site, so your browser does not contact any third party while you view it.

Data stored on your device

Only your light/dark preference, once you pick one, is kept in this browser's local storage, under home.theme. It is not transmitted anywhere and stays until you clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), whose servers log technical access data (your IP address, the time, the file requested, your user agent, and any referring page) to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This involves a transfer to the USA. Those logs are created and controlled by GitHub, not by the provider of this site, who has no access to them. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to apps

When you open one of the apps linked from this page, you leave this page. Each app has its own privacy policy, which you can find inside the app.

Your rights

The controller under Art. 4(7) GDPR is the provider named in the Impressum. You have the rights to information, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). In practice there is little to act on: no accounts or databases are kept here, and requests about the server logs above have to go to GitHub.
