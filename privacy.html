Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

This is a static website with no server component of its own, no accounts, and no login. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. All files, including the fonts, are served from this site, so your browser does not contact any third party while you view it.

Data stored on your device

Only your light/dark preference, once you pick one, is kept in this browser's local storage, under home.theme. It is not transmitted anywhere and stays until you clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), whose servers log technical access data (your IP address, the time, the file requested, your user agent, and any referring page) to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This involves a transfer to the USA. Those logs are created and controlled by GitHub, not by the provider of this site, who has no access to them. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to apps

When you open one of the apps linked from this page, you leave this page. Each app has its own privacy policy, which you can find inside the app.

Lulogo and Google Drive

Lulogo, the vector editor at alex-bre.github.io/lulogo, runs entirely in your browser and has no server component and no account of its own. Its File menu can optionally open files from your Google Drive and save projects and exports to it, and Drive's “Open with” can start Lulogo on a file. Only when you use one of these does the app load Google's sign-in and file-picker scripts from accounts.google.com and apis.google.com, and ask you to sign in with your Google account. Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) then processes your account data, IP address, and the files involved under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy, which may include a transfer to Google LLC in the USA (certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework). The legal basis is your request to use the feature (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Lulogo asks only for access to the files you open with it, pick in the Drive file picker, or create with it (drive.file), plus permission to appear under “Open with” in Drive (drive.install), and never for the rest of your Drive. It uses that access only to read the files you open and to write the files you save; it does not store, share, or transfer your Google data anywhere else. The access token Google issues stays in the memory of your browser tab and is gone when you close it. Files travel directly between your browser and Google, and no copy reaches the provider of this site. You can withdraw the access at any time in your Google account settings. Lulogo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

You have the rights to information, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). In practice there is little to act on: no accounts or databases are kept here, and requests about the server logs above have to go to GitHub.
